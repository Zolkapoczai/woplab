# RSM Hungary

RSM Hungary is a Accounting company. RSM Hungary provides expert advice in tax advisory, accounting, auditing, and payroll services. RSM Hungary employs 168 people (+15.1% YoY, +22 people), founded in 2001. Headquartered in Budapest, Hungary.

## About

RSM Hungary's core competences are accounting, tax advisory, payroll, audit and HR consulting combined with the most advanced digital solutions. The achievements of RSM Hungary founded in 2001 confirm that the values and beliefs of the founders of the company stand the test of time. The RSM Hungary has been offering audit services to its partners in Hungary through its audit firm since 2009. RSM Hungary Auditing Plc. is a member of the Hungarian Chamber of Auditors.
Our successes were achieved with the help of our colleagues who have identified with the values of the Hungarian owners: excellent expertise, continuous knowledge development, disciplined and diligent work, precision, business integrity, order and system. It is these values that helped our company in becoming an acknowledged player of the profession and of business life despite fierce market competition and at a time of crisis. We provide each client with the necessary information and expertise in the form and through the channel most suitable for them. 
Our firm joined RSM International in 2009. RSM International is the 6th largest international accounting and consulting network, comprising over 43 000 professionals with offices in 120 countries. With this, our clients benefit from a global pool of professional resources in their international transactions.

More information about RSM Hungary Plc. and up-to-date publications are available on our website: rsm.hu
For information on RSM International please visit: rsmi.com
